01 — About

A bit about me

I'm an Android developer based in Lahore, Pakistan, working mainly in Kotlin. Most of my time goes into building app UIs with Jetpack Compose, wiring them to clean, testable layers underneath, and keeping the codebase simple enough that the next feature doesn't fight the last one.

My side projects are where I try things out — Compose Multiplatform, Ktor, custom UI components, and small utilities I actually want on my own phone. You'll find most of them on GitHub, and a selection below.

Right now I'm at Imagination AI, and I'm open to interesting Android work — full-time or freelance.
